Data democracy is about making reliable information understandable and available to the people who need it. Access matters, but access alone is not enough: people also need clear definitions, useful context, and confidence in the source.

The goal is not to turn everyone into a data analyst. It is to help more people ask better questions, explore evidence, and take part in decisions that affect their work.

When organizations simplify access and improve trust in the data, teams move faster, align better, and make decisions with more clarity.
