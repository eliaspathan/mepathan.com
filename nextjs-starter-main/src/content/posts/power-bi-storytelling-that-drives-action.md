The best Power BI reports do not simply present metrics. They frame a decision, compare options, and lead the audience toward a meaningful next step.

Instead of overwhelming users with every field, focus on the few questions that matter most. Good design removes noise, highlights exceptions, and gives context to trend lines.

That is where reporting becomes decision support: clear visuals, strong narrative structure, and trust in the data behind the scenes.
