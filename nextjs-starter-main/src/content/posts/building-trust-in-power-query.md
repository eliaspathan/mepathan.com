Power Query is often the first place where data quality issues show up. A small inconsistency in a source system can lead to confusion across the whole report if the transformation logic is not documented and structured well.

Templates, consistent naming, and transparent steps help reduce friction. They also make it easier for teams to maintain and trust data products over time.

When the transformation process is easier to understand, the insights created from it become more credible and accessible to the wider team.
